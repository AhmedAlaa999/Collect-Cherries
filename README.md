# Collect Cherries

Collect Cherries is a fun and challenging 2D platformer where your goal is to collect **10 coins** while jumping between platforms. As the camera continuously scrolls upward, you must keep climbing, collect every coin, and avoid falling behind the screen.

Can you collect all 10 coins and reach victory before the camera leaves you behind?

## Gameplay

Jump between platforms, explore the vertical level, and collect coins as you climb higher. The camera constantly moves upward, making every jump important. Stay alert, collect all 10 coins, and reach the victory screen to complete your mission.

## How to Play

- Move left and right arrows to navigate the level.
- Press **Space** to jump.
- Jump between platforms to keep climbing.
- Collect all **10 coins** to win.
- Avoid falling behind the camera, or the **You Died** screen will appear.
- Reach the victory screen to complete the game!

### Mission

Your mission is simple: collect all 10 coins while surviving the continuously scrolling camera. Time your jumps carefully, land on platforms, and keep moving upward. Can you collect every coin without falling behind?

## Features

- Coin collection system.
- Coin counter tracking your progress toward 10 coins.
- Automatic upward-scrolling camera.
- Jumping and movement animations.
- Jump and coin collection sound effects.
- Victory screen when all 10 coins are collected.
- Death screen when the player falls behind the camera.
- Main menu and retry navigation.
- Pixel-art platforming gameplay.
- Developed using Godot Engine and GDScript.

## Technologies

- **Engine:** Godot Engine
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Genre:** 2D Platformer
